About Us

Arbor Vitae is a Christian student journal on the campus of the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor. Members from all disciplines and of diverse ages produce articles each semester for inclusion in our publication. During the school year, members meet weekly to discuss ideas, edit drafts, and create community. Though members hail from various denominations of Christianity and sometimes hold different convictions, we are united by the love of Christ and our desire to glorify him with the gift of writing. Arbor Vitae is a member of the Augustine Collective, a network of Christian student journals across the United States that provides us with resources and knowledge to improve our club.
